Stay Alert. Stay Secure.

Simple habits can help you protect your accounts, personal information and online experience.

01. Identify Spam

Watch for repeated unwanted messages, misleading offers, suspicious links and unexpected attachments. Avoid interacting with messages you do not trust.

02. Protect Your Accounts

Use strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication where available. Never share passwords or verification codes with strangers.

03. Recognize Scams

Be careful with urgent payment requests, fake prizes, impersonation attempts and offers that seem too good to be true. Verify claims through official channels.
